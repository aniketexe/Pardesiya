## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the sensory warmth of an archival field journal kept by an inquisitive traveler wandering through India. It rejects clinical, hyper-sanitized digital interfaces in favor of physical tactility, heritage craftsmanship, and curated editorial grandeur. The tone strikes a balance between vintage discovery and contemporary digital utility—evoking the texture of handmade Khadi paper, aged brass cartography instruments, sun-dried terracotta pottery, and postal dispatch ledgers.

The target audience comprises conscious cultural travelers, diaspora voyagers seeking connection, and heritage enthusiasts who value depth, storytelling, and local artisanal authenticity over generic tourism checklists. 

The aesthetic is **Tactile Editorial Heritage**. It leverages natural organic fiber textures, deckle-edge cutouts, stamp-inked metadata, warm aged parchment surfaces, and deliberate asymmetry. Digital interactions mimic the unhurried tactile pleasure of turning thick rag-paper folios, inspecting stamped wax seals, and pinning sepia-toned polaroids across an explorer's travel log.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from traditional Indian minerals, plant dyes, and raw materials:

- **Primary (`#A8441F` - Terracotta Rust):** An earthy, iron-rich fired clay red used for dominant calls to action, active navigation markers, key storytelling anchors, and prominent editorial highlights.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Warm Saffron Ochre):** A golden Marigold and haldi tone used for contextual highlights, energetic alerts, curated tags, rating indices, and decorative linework.
- **Tertiary (`#1E2B45` - Deep Royal Indigo):** A regal vegetable-dyed blue-black that grounds the system, used for high-contrast headers, formal postal stamps, state vector boundaries, and nightfall discovery states.
- **Neutral (`#706352` - Aged Raw Umber):** A mid-tone organic pigment used for soft structural rules, caption texts, metadata dividers, and secondary body accents.

### Surface System
Surfaces avoid stark `#FFFFFF`. The underlying canvas sits on `Parchment Base` (`#F8F4EA`), elevated through layered tiers of `Warm Kraft Paper` (`#EDE3D0`), `Raw Cardboard Container` (`#E2D5BE`), and `Deep Khadi` (`#D3C2A5`). High-contrast inverse layers utilize `Indigo Inkwell` (`#141E30`) with soft aged-bone text (`#F4EFE6`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates a deliberate tension between classical literary elegance and archival dispatch precision:

- **Headlines & Narrative Anchors (Playfair Display):** Rich in character, high-contrast transitional serifs provide majestic editorial presence for destination essays, historical monuments, and state showcases.
- **Body & Functional Content (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern humanist geometric sans-serif that balances readability across small viewports, ensuring effortless legibility against subtly textured paper surfaces.
- **Archival Metadata & Dispatch Stamps (Courier Prime):** Monospaced typewriter treatment reserved for field coordinates, journal entry dates, postal pass numbers, artisan guild registrations, and route markers.

Headings should embrace natural title casing and generous leading. Never force synthetic all-caps onto Playfair Display; reserve uppercase exclusively for `label-stamp` and `label-meta` to evoke ink-stamped field documentation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an archival 12-column grid system on desktop (`>1024px`) shifting to an 8-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1023px`) and a 4-column layout on mobile (`<767px`). 

Content spacing follows an unhurried, rhythmically paced journal spread. Unlike compressed corporate dashboards, Pardesiya breathes through deliberate asymmetrical gutters, generous top/bottom narrative pauses, and layered overlapping cards that break strictly rigid grid planes.

- **Outer Canvas Margins:** Desktop sections respect an expansive `margin` (`3rem` or `48px`), creating a natural mat-board framing around content. On mobile devices, this drops to `1.25rem` (`20px`) to maximize storytelling surface.
- **Grid Offsets:** Storytelling modules utilize staggered vertical offsets (e.g., alternating items shifted vertically by `space-xl`) to mimic photos casually laid across a study desk.
- **Component Padding Rhythm:** Internal card structures adhere tightly to `space-md` (`16px`) for utilitarian containers and `space-lg` (`28px`) for expansive editorial showcases.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through physical material layering rather than artificial glowing dropshadows or cold neon blurs:

1. **Warm Umber Ambient Cast:** All shadows carry an earthy, sepia-tinted undertone. Instead of neutral black shadows (`rgba(0,0,0,*)`), shadows utilize `rgba(74, 53, 33, 0.12)` to `rgba(74, 53, 33, 0.22)`.
2. **Layered Paper Tiers:**
   - **Ground Layer:** Screen canvas in textured ivory parchment (`#F8F4EA`).
   - **Tuck Layer (Elevation 1):** Kraft paper modules featuring a micro-emboss: a `1px` top/left highlight of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` and a bottom/right shadow of `1px 2px 4px rgba(60, 40, 20, 0.08)`.
   - **Artifact & Polaroid Layer (Elevation 2):** Floating gallery items, journal entries, and interactive cards cast a diffused physical lift: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(45, 30, 15, 0.14), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(45, 30, 15, 0.08)`.
   - **Modal / Seal Layer (Elevation 3):** Wax seals, postal stamps, and detail overlays feature sharp physical punch: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -6px rgba(30, 18, 8, 0.25), 0 0 0 1px rgba(168, 68, 31, 0.15)`.
3. **Deckle-Edge Masks:** Organic deckle margins are rendered via SVG mask borders (`rough-edge-mask`), visually separating primary parchment sections from kraft editorial modules without relying on standard CSS straight lines.

## Shapes

The shape architecture relies on a **Soft (`1`)** roundedness scale, representing hand-cut rag paper, pressed pulp boards, and stamped travel visas:

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Used for micro-components, metadata tags, input controls, and standard button containers to retain structured hand-trimmed paper geometry.
- **Card & Polaroid Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Applied to photo cards, interactive map tiles, and itinerary cards. The slight softening prevents digital harshness while preserving the feeling of physical cardstock.
- **Large Folio Radius (`0.75rem` / `12px`):** Reserved for large nested state drawers and primary feature panels.
- **Stamp & Seal Geometries:** Postal badges and artisan markers use circular serrated clip-paths (scalloped edges) or strict `90-degree` clipped corner bevels (`chamfered`) to maintain archival fidelity.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Terracotta Dispatch):** Solid `#A8441F` background, ivory parchment typography (`#F8F4EA`), `0.25rem` border radius, subtle debossed inner shadow (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`). Hover triggers a rich sepia warmth shift (`#913816`) with an intentional `1px` downward physical press.
- **Secondary Action (Waxed Indigo):** Deep `#1E2B45` outline (`1.5px`), off-white paper fill, indigo text, pairing with a monospaced label style.
- **Ghost / Artifact Trigger:** Text-only in Terracotta with a dotted terracotta baseline underline (`1.5px` offset by `4px`) and trailing archival directional symbol (`→`).

### Chips & Postal Tags
- Emulate authentic perforated postage stamps and luggage tags. Features a `1px` dashed amber-sepia border (`#D97706`), `Courier Prime` uppercase typography, and tiny circular punch-out notches on left/right edges rendered via CSS gradients.

### Cards & Curated Exhibits
- **Craft Paper Itinerary Card:** Textured warm kraft background (`#EDE3D0`) surrounded by a subtle `1px` dry-brush stroke (`rgba(112, 99, 82, 0.2)`). Includes an inset polaroid container with an asymmetrical rotation (`-1deg` to `+1.5deg`).
- **"Piece of Our Culture" Showcase:** Alternating two-column editorial split. One column features an oversized high-artisan photograph framed in deep indigo matting with brass pin accents; the adjacent column features a decorative initial drop-cap, an artisan guild origin stamp, and local material taxonomy.

### Interactive Cultural Map
- Vector-rendered state boundaries filled with muted parchment washes. Active and explored states highlight in warm Terracotta Rust with tactile stippled/crosshatch patterns. Hovering a region displays a weathered luggage-tag tooltip with regional crafts, climate indicators, and cultural festivals.

### Polaroid & Vintage Gallery Grid
- Photo frames feature heavy bottom borders (`32px` bottom margin vs `10px` top/sides) in antique ivory (`#FAF7F0`). Captions are styled in hand-annotated script or crisp `label-stamp` monospaced typewriter font, accompanied by miniature brass paperclip accents at top corners.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** Warm ivory surface (`#F4EFE6`) with an inset border simulating pressed paper indentations. Focus state switches border to `#A8441F` with an ambient ochre tint halo.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Modeled as stamped inventory boxes. Checked state displays a hand-inked cross (`✕`) or filled seal in Terracotta ink rather than a standard software tick.
